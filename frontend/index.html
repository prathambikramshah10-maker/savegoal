Your security matters

We take your privacy and data protection seriously.

🔐

Encrypted Passwords

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage. We never see your plain-text password.

🛡️

Secure Authentication

JWT tokens ensure your session is secure and only you can access your data.

👤

Data Isolation

Each user can only see their own goals and transactions. Your data stays yours.

🚫

No Money Transfers

SaveGoal is a tracking tool, not a bank. We never hold or transfer your money.
