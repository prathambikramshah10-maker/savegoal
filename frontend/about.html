About SaveGoal

Building better financial habits, one goal at a time

Our Mission

SaveGoal was built with one simple idea: everyone deserves an easy way to track their savings. We noticed that many people in Nepal struggle to manage their finances — not because they don't earn enough, but because they lack a simple tool to set goals and track progress.

That's why SaveGoal exists. A clean, private, and free platform where you can set savings goals, track how much you've saved, and celebrate when you reach your targets. All amounts are displayed in Nepalese Rupees (NPR) so everything feels familiar.

What We Believe

🎯

Clear Goals Lead to Success

When you know exactly what you're saving for and how much you need, you're more likely to stay committed.

🔒

Privacy is Non-Negotiable

Your financial data is personal. We never sell your information, and only you can see your goals and transactions.

💰

Free Forever

SaveGoal is and will always be free to use. No hidden fees, no premium tiers, no ads.

🇳🇵

Built for Nepal

Every amount is in NPR. We understand the local context and designed SaveGoal to work for Nepali users.

Technology

SaveGoal is built with modern web technologies. The frontend uses vanilla JavaScript with a responsive design that works on any device. The backend is powered by Node.js and MongoDB, deployed on Render. Your passwords are encrypted with bcrypt, and all API routes are secured with JWT authentication.
